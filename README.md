A pure HTML/CSS/JavaScript website for TCH's international UAV product presentation and direct sales contact.

- 8 languages: English, Chinese, Arabic, Spanish, French, Russian, Persian and Turkish
- RTL layout for Arabic and Persian
- 14 catalog entries based on the supplied product documents
- Real product imagery for UAV platforms, flight control, GNSS, propulsion, servo and ground station
- Product-category filtering and catalog search
- Click-to-enlarge product images
- Responsive mobile navigation
- Direct Email and WhatsApp contact; no fake inquiry backend
- Engineering/product visual panel
- International reach section
- Controlled/regulated product compliance gate with case-by-case review language
- Local language preference and essential-cookie dismissal

## Important product-data note

Product specifications shown on the site come from the supplied product documents and can vary by configuration. Confirm final configuration, supplier authorization, compliance requirements and export eligibility before quotation or transaction.

The public site does not publish operational weapon-use details. Controlled or regulated categories are routed to direct contact and case-by-case compliance review.
